WHY TYPEFLOW?

good tools get out of your way.

Typing is a small skill with a big daily impact. TypeFlow keeps the experience straightforward: choose a mode, type at your own pace, see your results, and come back tomorrow.

From everyday words to snippets of Python, C++, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS, the goal stays the same: accurate, comfortable typing practice, not learning to program or executing code.

SDG 4 calls for inclusive, equitable quality education and lifelong learning opportunities. TypeFlow supports the broader spirit of this goal through free, accessible, self-paced digital-skills practice.
